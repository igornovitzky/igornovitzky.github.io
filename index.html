Personal cloud backup configuration

A personal connection to cloud storage.

rclone photos is a personal configuration maintained by Igor for connecting his own Google account to rclone and protecting his files with cloud backups.

What the app does

The configuration uses the open-source rclone software to connect to Google Photos and Google Drive. Its backup workflow uses BorgBackup to create encrypted archives locally, then transfers the encrypted repository to the owner's Google Drive.

Google Drive access supports uploading backup files, listing the backup folder, checking file metadata and checksums, and maintaining the cloud copy. Google Photos access is used only for the owner's personal photo connection, subject to the functionality available through Google's Photos API.

This OAuth client is for its owner's personal use. This website explains the configuration and its data practices; Google account authorization takes place through Google's consent screen.

Privacy and contact

Read the privacy policy for details about account access, storage and retention, and the terms of use for the scope of this personal configuration. Contact the maintainer through the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
