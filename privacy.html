Privacy policy

This policy describes the personal rclone photos configuration maintained by Igor and its access to his own Google account.

Data accessed and purpose

When the owner authorizes Google Drive access, rclone can access file contents and metadata under the granted permissions. The backup workflow uses that access to upload encrypted backup files, list repository files, compare metadata and checksums, and maintain the backup copy. Google Photos authorization allows the personal photo connection to access the photo and media information permitted by Google's API and the granted scopes.

Google account data is used solely for these personal storage and backup functions. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train artificial intelligence models. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and protection

OAuth credentials and authorization tokens are stored on the owner's computers or server in access-restricted configuration files. Backup data is encrypted locally by BorgBackup before its encrypted repository is uploaded to the owner's Google Drive. Authorized rclone operations communicate with Google over HTTPS. Logs may contain filenames, metadata, transfer status and error details, and remain on the owner's systems.

Sharing and service providers

Google processes data required to provide Google Drive and Google Photos. The owner does not provide this configuration as a public service or grant unrelated people access to the backup data. This information website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process ordinary website request information under its privacy statement. These pages have no account form, analytics scripts or advertising integrations.

Retention and owner control

Backup snapshots are retained according to the owner's configured backup policy. Local retention currently keeps four weekly and six monthly snapshots; cloud cleanup follows the configured mirror workflow. Failed cloud operations can delay cleanup. The owner can delete backup files and revoke the app's access through Google Account permissions. Revoking access stops future authorized operations; it does not automatically delete backups already stored.

Contact and changes

Contact the maintainer using the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen. This policy will be updated if the configuration's data practices change.
